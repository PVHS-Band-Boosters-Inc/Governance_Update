# School District Relations and Facilities Policy
### PVHS Band Boosters, Inc. — DRAFT for adoption by the Board of Directors

## 1. Purpose

Operationalizes the St. Johns County School District's *Guidelines for School Support Organizations* and Bylaws Article XVI. The district's remedy for noncompliance is revocation of the authorization to use the school's name — with a five-business-day appeal to the Superintendent and, if revocation stands, a mandatory corporate name change. Treat every item here as existential, not administrative.

## 2. Annual Authorization and Submissions (owner: Treasurer, with Secretary)

Each year, and promptly upon change, obtain the Principal's written permission to use the school's name, logo, mascot, or trademark, and submit to the Principal:

| Item | Source |
|---|---|
| Current bylaws + any amendments (submit amendments promptly when adopted) | Guidelines §III |
| Annual budget | Guidelines §IV |
| List of Officers and list of check signers | Guidelines §IV |
| Annual report on the District's prescribed form | Guidelines §IV |
| Most recent Florida Dept. of State annual report | Guidelines §IV |
| Proof of liability insurance — or the no-GL-insurance member notification (Bylaws Art. XII) | Guidelines §X |
| Final bank statement for the period including June 30 | Guidelines §IV |
| Volunteer Liability Awareness questionnaire (officer/member liability-insurance disclosure with verification) | Guidelines appendix |

**Public records notice:** everything submitted to the school — including correspondence and email — may become a public record under Florida's Public Records Law (Guidelines §IV). Do not submit member personal data beyond what the Guidelines require.

## 3. Volunteer Registration

All Officers, Directors, and members register as School District volunteers (Guidelines §VIII; Bylaws Arts. IV–V) via the District's School Access Volunteer Application, which includes consent to a criminal background check and sex-offender check. District school access runs in three-year intervals — the Volunteer Committee tracks expiration dates, verifies Officer/Director registration at the start of each term and before any student-facing activity, and promotes member registration at annual enrollment. Volunteers present current state-issued ID at each school sign-in.

## 4. Facilities and Events on School Property

- File the District's facility-use form for every event on school property (Guidelines §VI).
- **Revenue share:** if a rental fee is charged, 6% is reserved for the District; if the fee is waived but participants pay, 6% of gross collections (less sales tax) is remitted to the District. Budget this into every event pro forma.
- If an event requires District staff outside regular hours, the corporation pays the applicable salary and benefit costs.
- **Alcohol:** no alcoholic beverages served or consumed at events on school property (School Board Rule 3.15). At off-campus events, follow the District's guidance memo — alcohol may not be provided by the corporation as part of admission or via cash bar.
- **Beverage exclusivity:** where the District has an exclusive beverage vendor agreement, only that vendor's products are sold.
- **Accessibility:** ensure individuals with disabilities have equal access to participate in activities and events; coordinate accommodations with the school and, as needed, the ESE department (Guidelines §§III, VI).

## 5. Independence

The corporation maintains its own EIN, bank accounts, and sales-tax posture (never the school's or Board's numbers); it does not obligate or incur debt in the name of the school, School Board, or District, and is not their partner or agent (Guidelines §§III, V, VII). Contracts state the corporation's own name.

## 6. Fundraising Coordination

Fundraising and solicitation comply with FDACS requirements under ch. 496, Fla. Stat. (Guidelines §III; Gift Acceptance Policy §6), and with school scheduling and the Principal's instructions. Use of the school's name or marks in fundraising materials falls under the Section 2 authorization.

## 7. If Revocation Is Threatened

Any notice of noncompliance goes to the full Board immediately. The five-business-day appeal window to the Superintendent is calendared on day one; counsel is engaged; and the Board treats cure-and-communicate as the first strategy.
