# Records Request and Financial Transparency Policy
### PVHS Band Boosters, Inc. — DRAFT for adoption by the Board of Directors

## 1. Purpose

Implements member inspection rights (§§617.1602–617.1604, Fla. Stat.), the financial-statement right (§617.1605), and director inspection rights (§617.16051). These member rights cannot be abolished or limited by the corporation's documents (§617.1602(6)); this Policy channels them, it does not restrict them.

## 2. Intake and Clock

All records demands go to the Secretary (governance records) or Treasurer (financial records), who log the date received. **The statutory clock is five (5) business days.** The responder confirms receipt within two (2) business days and identifies any information needed to process the request.

## 3. Tier 1 — Available on demand (§617.1602(1))

Articles; bylaws; member-meeting minutes and member written actions; written communications to members (past 3 years), including furnished financial statements; the current Directors-and-Officers list; the most recent annual report. Provided within 5 business days of the demand, no purpose statement required.

## 4. Tier 2 — Purpose-connected records (§617.1602(2)–(3))

Board/committee minute excerpts, accounting records, the membership list, and other books and records — available when the demand (a) is in good faith and for a proper purpose reasonably related to the member's interest as a member, (b) describes the purpose and the records with reasonable particularity, and (c) seeks records directly connected to that purpose. The disinterested Officers evaluate; denial reasons are documented in writing. Reasonable confidentiality, use, and distribution restrictions may be imposed (§617.1602(4)); copying costs may be charged at actual cost (§617.1603(3)).

## 5. Financial Statements (§617.1605)

**Standing practice:** the Treasurer posts the annual financial statements (year-end balance sheet and statement of operations) to the corporation's website within 90 days of fiscal year end — website posting satisfies the statute (§617.1605(3)) and moots most demands. On any written demand: if statements exist, deliver or point to the posting within 5 business days; if not yet prepared, notify within 5 business days and deliver within 60 days. **Missing these deadlines exposes the corporation to a mandatory attorney-fee award (§617.1605(6)) — treat them as hard deadlines.**

## 6. Membership List Safeguards

The member list is released only on a qualifying Tier 2 demand and never for commercial use, solicitation unrelated to member interests, or sale (§617.1602(9)–(10)); recipients acknowledge those limits in writing.

## 7. Directors

A Director's inspection request reasonably related to Board duties is honored at any reasonable time without the member-demand formalities (§617.16051). Refusal requires action of the disinterested Directors and a documented basis — an improperly refused director can obtain court-ordered inspection at the corporation's expense, plus fees.

## 8. Privacy Overlays

Student records, medical forms, and minors' information are redacted or withheld as required by district policy and applicable privacy law, documented in the response.
