# Whistleblower Policy
### PVHS Band Boosters, Inc. — DRAFT for adoption by the Board of Directors

## 1. Purpose

The corporation is committed to lawful and ethical conduct. This Policy provides channels for reporting concerns, protects reporters from retaliation, and implements the statutory reporting duty of Officers under section 617.0844(6), Florida Statutes.

## 2. Officers' Statutory Duty

Each Officer **must** inform the Board (or an appropriate superior, followed by the Board where the concern is material) of (a) material information about the corporation's affairs within the scope of the Officer's functions, and (b) **any actual or probable material violation of law involving the corporation, or material breach of duty to the corporation, by any officer, employee, or agent.** Casual mention to a colleague does not discharge this duty. An Officer who is unsure whether a matter is material errs on the side of reporting.

## 3. Who May Report; What to Report

Any Director, Officer, member, volunteer, student parent, or Program staff member may report, in good faith, suspected: financial irregularity or misappropriation; falsification of records; misuse of restricted or scholarship funds; violations of law (including tax, charitable-solicitation, or school-district requirements); safety concerns involving students; or violations of Booster policies.

## 4. How to Report

Reports may be made to the President, or — where the concern involves the President or any Director — to any other Director, who shall bring it to the disinterested Directors. Reports may be written or oral and may be made anonymously. **No report is ever routed through, or requires the approval of, a person implicated in it.**

## 5. Handling

The disinterested Directors shall acknowledge (if the reporter is known), assess, and investigate proportionately, using outside professionals where warranted, document the outcome, and report the disposition to the Board. Records of reports and investigations are retained per the Document Retention Policy.

## 6. Mandatory District Reporting

Any allegation of misappropriation or misuse of the corporation's funds is reported to the school Principal and the District's Director of Community Relations, as required by the District's Guidelines for School Support Organizations (§V), in addition to — never instead of — the internal handling above. The disinterested Directors make this report promptly; it is not contingent on the internal investigation's outcome.

## 7. No Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith, or participates in an investigation, is prohibited and is itself grounds for removal proceedings. Good-faith reports that prove unfounded carry no consequence for the reporter. Knowingly false reports are a violation of this Policy.

## 8. Confidentiality

Reports are handled confidentially to the extent consistent with an adequate investigation, the mandatory District reporting in Section 6, and legal obligations. Note that materials furnished to the school may become public records under Florida's Public Records Law (District Guidelines §IV).
