# Document Retention and Destruction Policy
### PVHS Band Boosters, Inc. — DRAFT for adoption by the Board of Directors

## 1. Purpose

Implements section 617.1601, Florida Statutes, and federal record-keeping expectations (Form 990 Part VI line 14). No document may be destroyed in anticipation of, or during, any investigation, audit, litigation, or records demand.

## 2. Retention Schedule

| Record | Retention | Statutory driver |
|---|---|---|
| Articles of incorporation, bylaws, amendments | Permanent | §617.1601(1)(a)–(b) |
| Board and committee minutes; written consents | Permanent | §617.1601(1)(d) |
| Member meeting minutes; member written actions | Minimum 3 years (retain permanently as practice) | §617.1601(1)(c) |
| Written communications to members generally, incl. financial statements furnished | 3 years | §617.1601(1)(e) |
| List of current Directors and Officers, with addresses | Current at all times | §617.1601(1)(f) |
| Most recent Dept. of State annual report | Current | §617.1601(1)(g) |
| Record of members (alphabetical, with contact info) | Current at all times | §617.1601(3) |
| Accounting records sufficient to prepare §617.1605 financial statements | 7 years | §617.1601(2); IRS practice |
| IRS determination letter; Forms 990 and supporting workpapers | Permanent (letter); 7 years (returns/workpapers) | IRS practice |
| Bank statements, reconciliations, invoices, receipts | 7 years | audit support |
| Contracts and staff/designer agreements | 7 years after expiration | §6672 exposure; audit support |
| Insurance policies | Permanent | claims-made tail |
| Charitable solicitation (Ch. 496) registrations | 7 years | FDACS |
| Volunteer screening and student medical/travel forms | Per district requirements; minimum duration of student enrollment + 3 years | district policy |
| Whistleblower reports and investigation records | 7 years after closure | oversight documentation |
| Annual conflict disclosure statements | 7 years | §617.0143 qualification evidence |

## 3. Format and Custody

Records may be kept electronically if convertible to usable form within a reasonable time (§617.1601(4)). The Secretary is custodian of governance records; the Treasurer of financial records. Custody transfers at each officer transition are documented in writing — records belong to the corporation, not the officer (see §617.0808(1)(g): 72-hour turnover on removal).

## 4. Destruction

When a retention period ends, records are destroyed securely (shredding; verified electronic deletion). Destruction is logged (what, when, by whom). The suspension rule in Section 1 overrides every schedule entry.
