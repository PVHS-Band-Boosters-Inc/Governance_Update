# Conflict of Interest Policy
### PVHS Band Boosters, Inc. — DRAFT for adoption by the Board of Directors

## 1. Purpose and Effectiveness

This Policy implements sections 617.0143 and 617.0832, Florida Statutes, and supports the corporation's federal tax-exempt status. **Phase-in:** Sections 3–5 (disclosure, recusal, documentation) are effective on adoption by the Board. Section 6 (approval of conflicted transactions) becomes operative only upon member adoption of revised Bylaws Article XI; until then, the corporation does not enter transactions in which a covered person has a material interest.

## 2. Covered Persons

Directors, Officers, committee chairs, the Band Director (in his or her dealings with the corporation), and family members of each (spouse; child, stepchild, parent, stepparent, grandparent, sibling, step- or half-sibling of the person or the person's spouse).

## 3. Duty to Disclose

A covered person shall disclose to the Board, promptly and before the corporation acts, all material facts of any transaction or arrangement in which the person has an actual or potential direct or indirect material financial or other interest, including interests held through a family member or an entity the person is affiliated with.

## 4. Recusal

The interested person shall not participate in Board deliberation or vote on the matter. The Board may ask the person questions before deliberation. The person's presence does not count toward approval.

## 5. Annual Disclosure Statement

Each Director and Officer shall sign the statement in Appendix A at the start of each term and upon any material change. The Secretary retains statements with the corporate records.

## 6. Approval of Conflicted Transactions *(operative upon Bylaws Article XI adoption)*

Before approval: (a) full disclosure recorded in minutes; (b) recusal; (c) written comparability information (quotes, market data, or comparable arrangements) obtained where reasonably available and retained; (d) authorization by a majority of qualified directors (per §617.0143) acting in good faith, with knowledge of the material facts, and in the corporation's best interest — never by a single director. The minutes record who was qualified, the information relied on, and the vote.

## 7. Prohibited Regardless of Disclosure

Loans to Directors or Officers (§617.0833, Fla. Stat.); acceptance of gratuities, perquisites, or gifts from proposed or current vendors; compensation of Officers for their service.

## 8. Violations

Suspected violations are addressed by the disinterested Directors, which may include voiding the transaction where permitted, corrective disclosure, or removal proceedings under the Bylaws.
