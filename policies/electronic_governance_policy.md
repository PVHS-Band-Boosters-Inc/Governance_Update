# Electronic Governance Policy
### PVHS Band Boosters, Inc. — DRAFT for adoption by the Board of Directors

## 1. Purpose

Makes the corporation's remote meetings, electronic votes, and electronically signed consents legally reliable under chapter 617, Florida Statutes.

## 2. Remote Meetings

Board and member meetings may be held wholly or partly by remote means through which all participants can simultaneously hear one another (§§617.0820(4), 617.0721). Remote participants are deemed present. For member meetings, the notice states the access method; the corporation implements reasonable identity verification (Section 4) and gives remote participants a reasonable opportunity to participate and vote.

## 3. Written Consents and Electronic Signatures

Board action without a meeting requires the signed consent of **all** Directors (§617.0821); member action without a meeting follows §617.0701(4). Electronic signatures (typed-name-plus-email confirmation, e-sign platform, or signed scan) are valid. Each consent states the action, the date of each signature, and is delivered to the Secretary; Board consents are effective when the last Director signs and delivers (a Director may revoke before then). All consents are filed with the minutes.

## 4. Identity Verification

For electronic member votes and remote participation: voting links or credentials are issued only to the member contact information in the corporate record; one credential per member; the Secretary certifies the tally. For Board consents: Officer email addresses of record or an e-sign platform with audit trail.

## 5. Records of Electronic Actions

The Secretary preserves, per the Document Retention Policy: meeting notices, attendance (including remote), consent instruments with signature timestamps, ballot tallies, and platform audit logs for contested or close votes.

## 6. Technology Baseline

Corporate accounts (not personal accounts) for any platform holding corporate records; access limited to current Officers; credentials rotated at transition.
