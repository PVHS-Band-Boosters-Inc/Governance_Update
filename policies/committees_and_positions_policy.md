# Committees and Individual Positions Policy
### PVHS Band Boosters, Inc. — DRAFT for adoption by the Board of Directors

## 1. Purpose and Authority

This Policy implements Bylaws Article VII. It is administrative, not corporate structure: the Board may amend, combine, rename, or discontinue any committee or individual position, and reassign chairs and reporting Directors, by resolution at any time, without member action, as Bylaws Article IX and Article VII provide.

## 2. Standing Committees and Individual Positions

Unless changed by Board resolution, the following committees and individual positions are established, with the duties and (where applicable) Officer-chair assignments below. Any committee not assigned an Officer-chair is led by a member — or, where the Board approves under Bylaws Article VII, another registered School District volunteer who is not a member — appointed by the Board.

| Committee / Position | Duties | Chair |
|---|---|---|
| Executive Committee | Consists of the five Officers; coordinates, between Board meetings, business the Board has authorized; exercises no authority reserved to the Board or the members (Bylaws Arts. III, VIII) | President |
| Budget and Finance Committee | Supports the Treasurer in budget preparation and monitoring and recommends to the Board, which retains sole and non-delegable authority over funds and budget (Bylaws Art. III) | Treasurer |
| Fundraising Committee | Oversees all fundraising activities and sponsorships of the Band and Color Guard | Vice President of Fundraising |
| Props Committee | Works in conjunction with the Band Director on the creation of props for the Band and Color Guard | Vice President of Operations |
| Friday Night Lights Committee | Oversees the needs of the Band for Friday night football games and set-up of all Band equipment in the stadium; coordinates with the PVHS Athletic Director and custodial staff for the Band food tent, seating, and game-day needs | Member appointed by the Board |
| Winter Guard and Indoor Percussion Committee | Oversees all needs and events of the Winter Guard and Indoor Percussion regiments, including uniforms, props, and travel | Member appointed by the Board |
| Transportation Committee | Oversees all transportation needs of the PVHS Band and Color Guard Programs | Member appointed by the Board |
| Food and Beverages Committee | Oversees all food and beverage needs for pregame (Friday Night) meals, competitions, and other events | Member appointed by the Board |
| Uniform Committee | Oversees all uniform needs of the Band and Color Guard programs during marching season | Member appointed by the Board |
| Volunteer Committee | Organizes all volunteer needs for Band events | Member appointed by the Board |
| Equipment and Instrument Committee | Ensures all equipment and instruments are in good working order and repair, and maintains rental agreements and the safe return of rented instruments at year end | Member appointed by the Board |
| Banquet Committee | Oversees and coordinates the end-of-year Banquet | Member appointed by the Board |
| Media Day Committee | Oversees all Media Day needs at the start of the school year | Member appointed by the Board |
| Social Media Committee | Maintains PVHS Band social media accounts | Member appointed by the Board |
| Alumni Coordinator *(individual position)* | Maintains the alumni database and invitations to alumni for Band events | Member appointed by the Board |
| Membership Committee | Maintains the list of Band Booster membership, assisting the Secretary under Article IV | Member appointed by the Board |
| Newsletter Committee | Maintains the Band newsletter and works with the Membership Committee to ensure information reaches the membership | Member appointed by the Board |
| Middle School Liaison *(individual position)* | Works with the Band Director on special events for local middle schools and communication with rising 9th graders and their parents | Member appointed by the Board |
| Senior Committee | Oversees Senior-year activities and needs | Member appointed by the Board |

## 3. Reporting

A committee chaired by an Officer reports through that Officer to the Board. Unless a different Director is designated by Board resolution, each other committee chair and individual-position holder reports to the Director assigned by the Board at the start of each Program year.

## 4. Restrictions

As provided in Bylaws Article VII, every committee chair, committee member, and individual-position holder is bound by:

- Bylaws Article V's prohibition on compensation and on accepting gratuities, perquisites, or gifts from proposed or current vendors; and
- Bylaws Article XI's conflict-of-interest disclosure and recusal requirements, before any transaction in which they or a family member has a direct or indirect material interest.

Committee chairs and individual-position holders additionally complete the annual disclosure statement in the Conflict of Interest Policy, at the start of each Program year and upon any material change. Ordinary committee members are not required to sign the annual statement but remain bound by the disclosure and recusal duties above.

## 5. Amendment

This Policy is adopted and amended by Board resolution. No member vote is required.
