# Schedule of Figures
### PVHS Band Boosters, Inc. — thresholds and rates referenced by the Fundraising and Federal Tax Compliance Policy

**Status.** This Schedule is **not adopted** by the Board and is not part of any policy. The **Treasurer maintains it**, updates it as figures change, and reports changes to the Board at the next meeting. Updating this Schedule is **not** an amendment of the Policy and requires no Board action (Policy §12).

**Where the Schedule conflicts with the governing statute, regulation, or current-year form instructions, those control.** Correct the Schedule; do not follow it against the source.

**Figures verified as of: 12 August 2026.**
**Next scheduled review: with the FY2027 budget, and before any approved drawing.**

## A. Indexed — re-check every year

These change without notice. Assume a figure in this block is stale if it has not been checked this calendar year.

| # | Figure | Current value | Applies to | Source of truth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Form W-2G reporting threshold | **$2,000** for payments made in calendar year 2026 | Raffles and drawings, together with the 300× multiple (A2) | Instructions for Forms W-2G and 5754, **current year** |
| A2 | "300 times the wager" multiple | 300× — not indexed | Applies together with A1; both tests must be met | Instructions for Forms W-2G and 5754 |
| A3 | Information-return threshold (Forms 1099-NEC / 1099-MISC) | **$2,000** for payments made after 31 Dec 2025; indexed from 2027 | Program staff, designers, and other service providers | IRC §6041(a), (h); OBBBA §70433 |
| A4 | IRC §6104(d) public-disclosure penalties | Per-day accrual against the individual responsible, plus a separate flat penalty for willful failure. **Amounts deliberately not recited** | Failure to provide returns or the exemption application | The year's IRS inflation-adjustment revenue procedure |

> **A1 and A3 come from the same statutory change.** If they ever appear to diverge here, one of them is wrong.

> **Do not use IRS Publication 3079 for A1.** It is revised 10-2018 and still shows the former $600 threshold. Topic publications are background; form instructions are authority.

> **A3 governs the filing obligation only.** It does not change the worker-classification analysis, does not change a recipient's own obligation to report the income, and does not license paying a worker below the threshold to avoid issuing a return. Confirm A3 each year rather than from memory -- it changed for payments made after 31 Dec 2025 and is now indexed. Where classification is contested or a record would be useful, the corporation may file voluntarily (Fundraising and Federal Tax Compliance Policy §8).
